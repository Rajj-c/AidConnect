FIELD SURVEY REPORT — VOLUNTEER NOTES
Date: 26 April 2026
Volunteer: Rajeswar Kumar
Area Visited: Rajaji Nagar, Near Old Bus Stand, Trichy

--- FINDINGS ---

1. Visited 3 streets in Rajaji Nagar between 9 AM and 1 PM.
   Total families surveyed: 47 families (approx 210 individuals)

2. FOOD SITUATION (Critical):
   - 23 families reported having NO food at home for the past 2 days
   - 8 families with children under 5 years are severely underfed
   - Local ration shop has been closed for 6 days due to owner illness
   - No alternative food source in the 2km radius

3. HEALTH SITUATION (High):
   - 11 elderly people reported they have run out of blood pressure and diabetes medicines
   - 2 children aged 4 and 7 have high fever, no access to doctor
   - 1 pregnant woman (8 months) has not had prenatal checkup in 2 months
   - No primary health center within walking distance

4. WATER & SANITATION (High):
   - Municipal water supply has been cut for 4 days
   - Families are using water from an open well — quality is questionable
   - 35 families have no toilet access, using open defecation
   - 3 cases of suspected diarrhea reported

5. SHELTER (Medium):
   - After last week's heavy rains, 6 houses have partial roof damage
   - 2 families are sleeping in the open after complete roof collapse
   - No tarpaulin/material provided so far

6. CHILDREN (High):
   - 67 children have not attended school for 1 week due to lack of food and illness
   - Children visibly weak and malnourished in affected households

--- MOST URGENT ACTION NEEDED ---
- Immediate food distribution for 23 zero-food families
- Medical team to visit elderly and sick children TODAY
- Water tanker needed immediately
- Medicines: BP tablets, diabetes medication, paracetamol
