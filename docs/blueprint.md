# **App Name**: AidConnect

## Core Features:

- Field Data Submission Portal: Provide a secure web form interface for field workers and NGOs to easily submit various types of ground-level data and reports.
- Document Digitization with OCR: Utilize OCR to scan and convert physical paper surveys and field reports into digital text, making previously scattered and non-digitized information accessible.
- AI-Powered Need Prioritization: Employ an AI tool to analyze collected data (including OCR'd text) to identify urgent community needs, categorize issues (food, health, education), and assign priority levels (high, medium, low) based on severity and affected population.
- Intelligent Volunteer Matching: Implement an AI tool to match available volunteers with specific tasks and locations based on their skills, availability, and geographical proximity to the high-priority needs.
- Real-time Operations Dashboard: Present an interactive dashboard with a map view to visualize prioritized needs, track volunteer locations, monitor task assignments, and provide NGOs with actionable insights into ongoing operations.
- Automated Alert System: Send timely notifications and alerts to volunteers about new tasks in their vicinity and to NGOs about newly detected high-priority issues or successful task completions.
- Role-based Access & Data Security: Implement secure, role-based access control (Admin, NGO, Volunteer) and encrypted data handling to protect sensitive community information.
- Offline Data Collection & Synchronization: Enable field workers to submit forms and data even without internet access, with automatic synchronization when connectivity is restored.
- Multilingual User Interface: Provide support for multiple local languages (e.g., Telugu, Tamil, Hindi, Malayalam, Kannada) to enhance accessibility for diverse users.
- Impact Measurement & Performance Analytics: Track key metrics such as number of people helped, tasks completed, and response times to evaluate and report on the effectiveness of aid efforts.
- Explainable AI Insights: Provide transparent explanations from an AI tool for why a particular need is prioritized or why a specific volunteer was matched, enhancing trust and understanding.
- Continuous Feedback Loop: Allow volunteers to mark task completion and provide feedback on success, and enable NGOs to rate the effectiveness of interventions, feeding into system improvement.
- Emergency Response Override: Activate a special emergency mode during disasters (e.g., floods, earthquakes) to override normal prioritization, auto-prioritize life-critical needs, and streamline urgent resource allocation.

## Style Guidelines:

- Color scheme is light, conveying professionalism and clarity.
- Primary brand color: Trusty Blue (#1566ED) for reliability and data clarity. (HSL: H=210, S=70%, L=50%)
- Background color: Serene Sky (#E8F1F8) to maintain focus on content. (HSL: H=210, S=20%, L=95%)
- Accent color: Vibrant Green (#33B233) to highlight calls to action and signify positive impact. (HSL: H=130, S=70%, L=40%)
- Headlines and prominent text will use 'Space Grotesk' (sans-serif) for a modern, tech-forward, and clear impression.
- Body text and detailed information will use 'Inter' (sans-serif) for high readability and a neutral, objective feel.
- Utilize simple, clean, and modern vector-based icons that are universally understood and visually consistent with data representation and coordination themes.
- The layout should be modular and responsive, prioritizing clear hierarchy for data presentation on dashboards and intuitive form navigation on all device sizes.
- Incorporate subtle, functional animations for data updates, priority changes, and notification pop-ups to provide clear visual feedback to the user without distraction.
